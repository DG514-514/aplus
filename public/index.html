FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who are your cleaners?

Every A+ team member is vetted and trained on our dorm-cleaning checklist before their first visit, so you get the same standard every time.

Do I need to be in my room during the clean?

No. Many clients schedule cleans while they’re in class. We’ll coordinate access with you (and your residence, where required) when you book.

Can I pause my plan over exams or breaks?

Yes. Just let us know ahead of time and we’ll skip visits during reading week, holidays or exam season.

What’s included in a standard dorm clean?

Dusting and wiping surfaces, desks and sills; vacuuming and mopping floors; emptying bins; making the bed; and tidying shared areas included in your plan.

How do I see my past and upcoming cleans?

Once you book, we’ll set up your client login. Sign in at any time to see your full order history, upcoming visits and totals.

Can a parent book on behalf of a student?

Absolutely. Parents can book and manage a plan — just include your student’s residence and room number when you reach out.
